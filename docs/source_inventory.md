## EN workbook (`20260511 Cash and Cash Equivalents EN.xlsx`)

19 sheets. Visible: `Cover`, `Executive Summary`, `Cash`, `TBills`, `HISA`,
`Cashable & Term Deposits`, `GIC 1yr-5yr`, `Data Lists`. Hidden: `Table of Contents`,
`Securities Team`, `Cash and equivalents`, `Bankers Acceptance`, `Money Market Funds`,
`BA Calculator`, `IGSI F.I. Calculator`, `Par Value Finder`, `IGSI Max Spreads`,
`Tapering Schedule`, `Ratings`.

Defined names: `Client`, `Date` (both anchored on `Cover`), `WealthPlanningSpecialist`, `web`, `WPS`.
`Date` is the single master report-date cell (`Cover!I29`); every other sheet's date display
(`HISA!G1`, `TBills!G1`, `Money Market Funds!G1`, `Executive Summary!P1` via `Cover!I29`) is a
formula referencing it, directly or indirectly. Writing one cell propagates everywhere.

Print areas are set on all visible/printable sheets. Conditional formatting is present on
`Cashable & Term Deposits`, `GIC 1yr-5yr`, `Bankers Acceptance`, `IGSI F.I. Calculator` (heat-style
formatting over the rate columns — must survive cell writes since openpyxl/COM writes preserve
existing formatting when only `.value` is set). One embedded WMF image on `Cover` could not be
read by openpyxl (dropped with a warning) — this is expected and does not block writes; it is the
IG Wealth Management logo and is never touched by the pipeline.

**Correction after deeper inspection (see `docs/workbook_mapping.md`): coordinates are *not*
uniformly identical between EN and FR.** `Cashable & Term Deposits`, `GIC 1yr-5yr`, `TBills`
date cells, and `Cover!I29` (report date) are coordinate-identical with plain numeric `Decimal`
values, so the French Output Responsibility can write the same canonical value straight through.
But `Cash` (Prime/Fed Funds) and `HISA` (yield) store the value as a **literal pre-formatted
French-locale text string** (`"4,45%"`, `"1,80%"`) rather than a number, and `Cash`'s label/value
cells sit at different coordinates than EN (`E31/E32` and `H31/H32`, vs EN's `D30/D31` and
`H30/H31`). `TBills`' rate cells (as opposed to its maturity-date cells) are also French text
(`"2,23%"`), just at EN-identical coordinates. See `docs/workbook_mapping.md` and
`config/workbook_map_fr.yaml` for the verified, cell-by-cell breakdown and the `value_format` flag
that tells the renderer whether to write a `Decimal` or a formatted French percent string.

Three "payout frequency" blocks in one sheet, each a 12-provider table in the fixed order
**B2B, BMO, BMO Mortgage GIC, BMO Trust Company, Bank of Nova Scotia, Equitable Bank, Equitable
Trust, Home Bank GIC, Home Trust Company, Laurentian Bank, Manulife Bank, National Bank**:

| Compound | 43 | 45–56 | E:I (1yr–5yr) |

| Cashables (1yr term) | 88 | 89–96 (8 providers only — no Laurentian/Manulife/National) | E (single "Annual" column) |

All rates are stored as bare numbers (`3.3` meaning 3.30%), driven by `INDEX/MATCH` formulas
against a `Data` sheet. `#N/A` appears for providers with no current cashable rate (Home Bank,
Home Trust in this snapshot) and must never overwrite a report cell. Every cell was individually
verified against the master prompt's claimed ranges — see `docs/workbook_mapping.md` for the
row-by-row confirmation, including two ranges (`E26:I37`, `E89:E96`) whose block identity is not
obvious without reading the term-type header cells (`H6`/`H23`/`H42`, `B86`).

## `NBF T bill rates.pdf`

Single page, fixed-width text table (not a proper table object — plain text layout). Confirmed
structure via `page.get_text()`:

```
26/06/17  2.23  99.756    A03AS5    ...   26/06/16  3.52   (US bills header nearby)
26/07/15  2.23  99.586    A03AS7        26/07/14  3.51
26/08/12  2.24  99.414    A03AT2        26/08/13  3.51
26/10/21  2.35  98.943    A03AW5        26/10/08  3.50
27/04/21  2.59  97.590    A03AW6        27/04/15  3.52
```

CAD rows carry `date (YY/MM/DD), yield%, price, identifier` plus a "days to maturity" figure printed
by the source itself (`40`, `68`, `96`, `166`, `348`) which the parser does **not** trust — see
Treasury-Bill responsibility, which recomputes `term_days` from `maturity_date - report_date`.
US rows carry only `date, yield%` (no price/identifier).

**This file is a current snapshot, not the literal May-11 source.** Its own footer prints
`rates issued on 05/08/26 07:47:12` — August 5, 2026, close to this session's "today" (2026-08-13),
not May 2026. Four of its five CAD rates/dates and four of five US rates/dates happen to numerically
match the master prompt's May-11 historical fixture (§17) exactly, and the fifth (US row 1) is off
by one digit: the PDF text reads `26/06/16` (2026-06-16) where the fixture and the already-published
`20260511 Cash and Cash Equivalents EN.xlsx!TBills!A24` cell both say 2026-06-06. Verified character-
by-character via `page.get_text()` — this is the real text in the file, not a parsing artifact.
Rather than "correct" the parser's output to match the fixture, `parsers/nbf_tbill.py` parses the PDF
faithfully; the historical regression test (§17) validates against the **already-published May 11
workbook's own cells**, not against a fresh parse of this (differently-dated) PDF — matching the
master prompt's own instruction that the May-11 numbers are a frozen fixture, not something a live
run is expected to reproduce from today's sources.
